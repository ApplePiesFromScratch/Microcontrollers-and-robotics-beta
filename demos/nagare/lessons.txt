Shared: the heater and the scale of warmth
WHAT    : both sides read the same heater cells and the same warmth scale, so the screen compares rules, not inputs.
@ /* ================================================================ OURS: hand-ons only */
OURS: every change is a hand-on between neighbours
HOLDING : each place holds M (stuff), PX and PY (carried rate as momentum) and Q (warmth), all whole numbers.
LETTERS : MOVE: a place's packet goes where its rate takes it, split over the four places it lands on (bilinear), the
          leftover unit to the biggest share, so the split is exact. Reflection at walls is booked as momentum the wall gave.
          PUSH: crowding on both sides of a face becomes rate (KP). DRAG: neighbours trade rate (KN). EXCHANGE: crowding
          handed down its own difference (KX), which kills the checkerboard a collocated grid makes.
          LIFT: a place warmer than the box as a whole is handed upward rate, cooler ones downward; the ground's give is counted.
          FEED and VENT: counted in and out.
WHY     : each letter moves a kept quantity from one place to another. Nothing is set. So the books are FORCED by construction
          and the step is a trade, not dt.
FOUND   : faces read in sweep order biased the flow; faces now read from a snapshot (all trades from one state).
FOUND   : LIFT against each neighbour shredded plumes; LIFT against the box mean (the context) does not.
TRAP    : qdiv divides in 32 bits when the operands are proven to fit and in 64 otherwise. A 64-bit divide by a variable is a
          library call on this chip; ten a place a latch cost 40 ms (A3).
TRY     : in a face trade, give the receiver one unit more than the giver loses. Predict: N1 fails at latch 1; the books
          line on the glass inverts. That is N4's control, written the other way.
@ /* ================================================================ TEXT: the textbook, stable fluids in Q16 */
TEXT: the textbook, as taught, in Q16
WHAT    : Stam's stable fluids: body force (lift), viscous diffusion, pressure projection by 20 Jacobi sweeps for
          continuity, semi-Lagrangian advection, heat advected and conducted. DT 1/4, BETA 1/4, NU 1/50, KAPPA 1/40.
          Values are Q16 fixed point (65536 = 1).
FOUND   : the floor-rounded multiply drained heat every step. qmul now rounds to nearest; a double-precision desk run
          (desk/text_double.c) agrees with this Q16 run to about a percent.
READING : its continuity residual and heat-book gap are measured and printed, not judged (N5). It is the reference method,
          and its books are open by design: projection is iterative and advection interpolates.
TRAP    : i0 * ONE, not i0 << 16: a left shift of a negative number is undefined in C (UBSan found it).
@ /* ================================================================ a puff
A puff: the same disc of warmth on both sides, booked as fed
WHAT    : a disc of warmth laid at the floor. It exists because N2 FAILED as written: with a steady heater, ours' lift
          (against the box as a whole) pools heat at the floor. The failure stays in the record (A1). The scene changed to puffs.
TIER    : KL = 24 was chosen after looking (A2). It is a tuning, named as one. Neither side's pace is a result.

WHAT    : the left pane writes continuity, Navier-Stokes and the energy equation on the glass; extra glyphs draw the
          partial, nabla, dot, squared, rho, nu and kappa. The right pane writes the letters instead.

Readings the board gives
WHAT    : buttons, stick and a cycle counter come in through three functions (section 13). The core never touches a pin.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the frame */
The frame: one step of each side, then the picture
WHAT    : views of warmth, speed and turning (circulation round small loops), read from each side's state at the same cut.
CUT     : turning is read round 4 x 4 loops. A different loop size reads a different turning (FLOWING: a turning holds over a
          band of cuts). Say the loop size with every turning number.
FOUND   : per-dot divisions in the drawing became tables made once (A4); the screen's print over the tour is the witness that
          the picture did not change.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- hands and the demo hand */
Hands and the demo hand
WHAT    : A a puff at the marker, B heater on/off, C the view, stick the marker, press restarts. 30 s untouched: the demo
          hand plays, the same on desk, emulator and board.
TRAP    : nagare_reset must reset every latch the controls read (ours_latch was not reset, so N4's control never fired once).
@ ---------------------------------------------------------------- the panel's wire
The wire and the change-only glass
WHAT    : the panel is sent only the tiles that changed (OS 4, IRORI). glass_forget marks all tiles stale after a reset.
@ ---------------------------------------------------------------- the readings NAGARE asks for
The readings NAGARE asks for
WHAT    : hal_buttons, hal_stick, hal_cycles are the only three things the board gives the core. A desk or emulator build
          supplies its own three, which is why the same core runs in three places and prints the same lines.
