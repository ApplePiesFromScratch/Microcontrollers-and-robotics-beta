# MINAMO (ESP32-C3 + ST7789). Lessons are inserted above each anchor by tools/lessons.py.
@ // MINAMO (水面, the water's face)
LESSON MAP: MINAMO, a wave that runs back to the last bit, filmed
SKILL      : the run-back rule on a field (160 x 120 heights); a push read from the step's number alone; a witness
             written on the way out and checked on the way back; a film with no clock variable.
BENCH      : cd bench && gcc -O2 -w -o /tmp/twin desk/twin.c && FRAMELINES=1 /tmp/twin 600 x | tail
             desk == emulated chip: bash bench/tools/check_c3.sh 600   (needs MCR_X, see docs/BOARD_MAPPING.md section 6)
READ FIRST : demos/minamo/README.md; docs/METHOD.md section 7; practice/p04_run_back (do it before reading section 7 here).
SECTIONS   : 2 types | 3 the mouth (DMA queue) | 4 state | 5 tools | 6 the film | 7 sources | 8 THE STEP | 9 light
             10 paintings | 11 koi | 12 band from above | 13 band from low | 14 overlays | 15 the latch | 16 run
THE EQUATION: h(s+1) = 2 h(s) - h(s-1) + F(h(s)) + S(s), and the same line solved for h(s-1) is the step back.
             On the board: every second latch of the way back must equal the print written at that latch on the way out,
             and the last latch must be flat water.
@ // ---------------------------------------------------------------- every type first
Types first
WHAT    : every struct sits above the first function (the IDE's prototype step; CI 63). tools/make_c3.py builds this one
          file from minamo.h and HIGH NOON's board mouth.
@ // ---------------------------------------------------------------- the mouth: FAREY's queue
The mouth: a DMA queue to the panel
WHAT    : wire_cmd and wire_data queue SPI transfers; the CPU draws the next band while the last one goes down the wire.
BUDGET  : 153,600 bytes a frame at 80 MHz is about 15.4 ms of wire. The wire runs beside the drawing, so a frame costs
          the larger of the two, not their sum (README: emulator counts).
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the state */
State: two height fields and a position on the film
HOLDING : WA and WB are int16 heights on a 160 x 120 grid. wold and wnew point at them; a step swaps the pointers.
WHAT    : film_p is a position on the film (a count); sim_s is the step the pond is at. Nothing reads a clock.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- whole-number tools */
Whole-number tools
WHAT    : grow_ring builds the turning table once; cosq and sinq read it (a turn is 65,536 parts). root is a whole-number
          square root by the carry. hsh is the stated scatter: drop i always lands at the same place.
TRAP    : these tools are used to place things and paint them. Inside the step only F and S appear (section 8).
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the film: a position
The film: the step is read from the position
WHAT    : step_at(p) rises 2 steps a latch to SMAX, holds, then falls 2 a latch back to 0. The film runs forward and the pond
          runs out and then back, because the step it must be at is a function of the film's position.
RELATION: playing the film backward (button C) is the same rule: move_film(-1) asks for step_at(p - 1).
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- what falls on the water
Sources: every push is read from the step number alone
WHAT    : rain_cum(t) is the count of drops landed before step t (in 1/256 drop), a rate rising from 0 to 3 a step, then held.
          Drop i lands at step t when drops_from(t) <= i < drops_from(t+1). Its place comes from hsh(i), never from a draw.
WHY     : the push S(t) must be the same going out and coming back. A random draw would not be: it advances a generator.
          A function of t is the same every time t is asked for (CI 139: an input looked up by a different label going back).
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the step: one function, both ways */
THE STEP: one function, both ways
WHAT    : leap(X, Y, t) writes X <- 2Y - X + F(Y) + S(t). Forward X holds h(s-1); back X holds h(s+1). F is the nine-point
          Laplacian times a speed that slows toward the banks, landed toward zero. It reads only Y.
WHY     : solved for the other end, the same line is the inverse, exactly, for ANY F of Y, rounding included, because F(Y) is
          added one way and the same F(Y) is in the line the other way. int16 wraps are a bijection too.
KICK    : a drop is a 7 x 7 kick whose middle is set so the whole kick sums to zero. A koi wake is a push ahead and a pull
          behind, also summing to zero, so no drop adds or removes water.
TRY     : make F read X as well as Y (add r[x] - o[x] inside L). Predict: the way back fails at the first witness;
          mirror_bad rises on the board's line. That is practice/p04's planted fault, here on a pond.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the light, by position on the film
Light by position on the film
WHAT    : light_at reads the film position and blends five lights of one painting (dusk, night, rain, storm, dawn).
          A tone map is grown once (grow_tm). The look changes with the position, never with a timer.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the paintings
The paintings: the floor once, the sky at each cut
WHAT    : paint_floor at boot; paint_sky when the shot changes. Lightning is painted into the sky by the film position.
BUDGET  : painting once and reading many times is the trade every frame here makes: memory for work.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- koi: tested where the bent ray lands
Koi: tested where the bent ray lands
WHAT    : a ray from the eye bends at the surface by the surface's slope (a reading's rate slot: the height difference).
          The koi is tested where the bent ray reaches the floor. No refraction formula with an angle is written.
RELATION: the slope is the rate of the height reading across the grid. The bend is a lever on it: value plus rate times move.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- seen from above: one band */
From above: one band of 16 rows
WHAT    : band_above reads heights under each dot, bends to the floor, takes sky in the surface by the slope, and adds the floor's
          light gathered by the curve (the second difference). One band is drawn while the last band goes down the wire.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- from low over the water: one band */
From low over the water
WHAT    : band_low is the low camera: rows far away read cells near the horizon. The same heights, a second cut of the same water.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- things in the air and on the water
Things in the air: the leaf, the drops, the glow
WHAT    : the leaf and rain are drawn where leaf_at(t) and drop_place(i) say. They are readings of the same rules the
          sources used, so the drawn drop lands where the kick went in.
@ /* ---------------------------------------------------------------- the latch */
The latch, and the witness
WHAT    : move_film steps the pond until sim_s equals step_at(film_p). witness() writes the pond's print every second step on
          the way out (PRINT[i]) and compares on the way back: mirror_ok or mirror_bad. At film position 0, pond_flat() is
          checked: homes or home_bad.
BENCH   : README record: 1,243 of 1,243 latches matched their print, desk and emulated chip; homes 1, bad 0.
          Rerun: FRAMELINES=1 /tmp/twin 2501 x | tail -3 (the twin prints homes, bad and mirror counts).
TRAP    : the witness compares a print, a hash. Two different ponds can share a print (1 in 2^32 per compare). The final check,
          pond_flat, compares every cell.
@ ---------------------------------------------------------------- run
Run
WHAT    : setup grows the tables and paints the floor; loop calls frame(). Button C (GPIO 21) flips play.
