**N1. Part IV, Unit 9, Demonstration 2.** "Time to zero is the reciprocal of that, with the sign kept: 1 / (4/5) = 5/4."
The reciprocal of grow = −4/5 with its sign kept is −5/4; the meeting is at +5/4 because the count of latches until a value
runs out is −value / rate (METHOD section 7), that is, minus the reciprocal of grow. The worked line uses 4/5 without its
sign and lands on the right number. *Proposal:* "Time to zero is minus the reciprocal of that: −1 / (−4/5) = 5/4."

build needs an x it is a count of latches. *Proposal:* a bridge box after Unit 9 pointing to Part IV's Unit 9 (when two
movers meet, as a count) and Part V's Unit V1.
