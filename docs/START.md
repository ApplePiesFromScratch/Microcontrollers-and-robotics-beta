## The core understanding, in one paragraph

At x = 3, `rate(x·x, x)` reads 6 whether x is seeded with rate 1, 7/2 or −1, while the rate slot inside x·x reads 6,
21 and −6. The slot's number belongs to the cut you chose; the answer belongs to the relation. The method is the habit of
telling those two apart before building on either, and of keeping books that show nothing was made or lost on the way.
(`docs/METHOD.md` section 3; `python3 docs/teach.py`, lesson 1.)
